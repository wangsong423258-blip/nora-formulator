# Nora attribution

Community use is free for personal and company commercial use, including local deployment and offline calculation. Its Runtime license requires a reasonably visible attribution:

- English: **Powered by Nora · PalEcho**
- 中文：**由 Nora · PalEcho 提供支持**

Suitable locations include the Nora feature, formulation result, About, Settings or technical support page. A large home-page placement is not required. Keep the wording legible and readily discoverable to the product's users. For a headless integration, place it in user-facing integration documentation, the administration interface or support material available to its users.

The SDK offers `createAttribution({ document, locale })`, `attributionText`, and `requiresAttribution(status)`. These helpers provide ordinary visible text and license-status guidance. They do not track users, inject advertisements, inspect third-party UI or prevent customization of Apache-licensed SDK code. The obligation arises from the separate Community Runtime license.

A separately granted paid Commercial / Enterprise authorization can waive this requirement. The Runtime reports the obligation from the signed license. A missing or unverified status does not establish a waiver.
